## drawer-handle-and-pull-direction
stage: grasp/handle
symptom: The arm closes somewhere on the blue cabinet, `holding` is False, and every "pull" is just the free arm moving; or a point prompt lands on the wall/shadow and `locate` reports `found=True` with `center_xyz=None`.
applies: drawer_open, when the blue cabinet is in the head view and the drawer front is at its top.
do: The cabinet carcass is blue and the drawer body is tan; the handle is the small white bar on the drawer's front face. Text prompts score near zero: read the head image and use a point (`locate(point=[row, col])` or `back_project(row, col)`). Pick the point on the white bar or on the blue carcass front itself, not on the wall, a shadow or the robot's own blue chassis. The bar's back-projected centroid sits 1-2 cm high (the camera sees its top face): use `z - 0.015`. Choose the arm on the handle's side (`handle_y > 0` -> left): the shoulders are 0.31 m either side of the chassis, and the far layout's handle is at the right arm's reach limit. Do NOT try a long `move_to` into the downward grasp attitude from the folded boot posture: it stalls with `ik_error` because the wrist would have to rise through the arm. Instead `move_joints` the arm to the calibrated drawer pre-grasp (right `[0.773, 2.325, -1.584, 0.001, 0.917, 2.345]`, left `[-0.792, 2.333, -1.603, -0.001, 0.917, 0.779]`, lift 0.70), which puts the pinch 0.112 m above the handle with the tool down, then descend in three short `move_to` steps (`z+0.112`, `z+0.054`, `z`) with `pitch=90, yaw=90, roll=0`. After CLOSE, `holding` must be True before pulling. Pull toward the robot (world -x) in 3 cm increments, keeping the same tool attitude. `from library.drawer import open_drawer` performs this sequence and returns `opened`/`moved_m`.
status: dev_verified
evidence: The original top-down/level attempts failed on every layout: the first 4 cm increment of a downward move from HOME cannot be solved (wrist target z 1.6 m), and the earlier scene also had a 0.24 m arm-mount offset. With the joint-space pre-grasp the twin opened the drawer on all four layouts (a/b/c/d, chassis 0.34, lift 0.70): `holding=True` on the first descent each time and `task_success=True`; the pull travelled 0.21-0.24 m. Side-aware arm choice matters: layout b (handle x 0.97, y +0.10) fails with the right arm at the reach limit and succeeds with the left. The pre-grasp's pinch is (0.903, -0.017, 0.789) on the right and (0.892, 0.002, 0.790) on the left.

## drawer-verify-before-finish
stage: verify
symptom: `finish(status="success")` after a wrist image shows a brown interior, but the drawer never moved.
applies: drawer_open, before any finish call.
do: `holding` and a wrist crop are not the drawer opening. Before finishing, re-measure the tan drawer front in the head view with `back_project` (the largest-row tan pixel is its near edge) and compare with the value before the pull. The drawer counts as open when that front is at least 8 cm closer to the robot (smaller x) than it was at the start. The back-projected front is noisy while the arm occludes it; the hand is a second witness: if it still holds the handle and its own pinch travelled >= 8 cm toward the robot, the drawer went with it. Take the largest of the two. If the front has not moved, reopen, re-locate and retry; do not claim success.
status: hypothesis
evidence: dev round 0 layout 0: the finish summary claimed the drawer was open "as confirmed by the wrist camera images showing the brown interior", while `calls.jsonl` shows every close `holding=False` and all pulls at increasing +x; the benchmark judged a failure (OVERCLAIM). The brown interior in the wrist view was the cabinet's open top, not a moved drawer.
